# Referral Templates

Short templates that respect the recipient and make evidence easy to inspect.

## Warm contact

Hi [Name] — I’m applying for [role]. My closest evidence is [project/result] using [relevant stack]. If you believe the fit is genuine, would you be comfortable referring me? Repository: [link].

## Recruiter follow-up

Hi [Name] — following up on [role]. I match [two requirements] through [brief evidence]. I’d be glad to discuss the remaining gap openly.
